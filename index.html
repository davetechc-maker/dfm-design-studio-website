ABOUT DFM DESIGN STUDIO

Creating spaces that work beautifully and endure.

DFM Design Studio is a Kenyan multidisciplinary design, construction and project consultancy delivering considered built environments across Kenya and East Africa.

We combine creativity, technical coordination and practical site experience to turn ideas into spaces that are functional, elegant and built to endure. Our concept-to-completion approach connects briefing, design, cost planning, fabrication, fit-out, construction, supervision and handover.

Clients can appoint DFM for a single discipline or an integrated concept-to-completion package.
